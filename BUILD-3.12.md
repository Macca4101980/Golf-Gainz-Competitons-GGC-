# GGC Build 3.12 — Society Membership Management
- Owner/Admin can remove members without deleting historic scores/account.
- Members can leave a society.
- Owner cannot leave until ownership is transferred.
- Owner can transfer ownership to another current member.
- Owner/Admin can promote/demote Admins.
- Invite Existing Golfer finds golfers shared through another society and sends a pending invitation.
- Recipient sees Group Invitations with Accept/Decline; membership is added only on Accept.
- Current user's member card is highlighted and labelled YOU.
- Retains 3.11/3.11.1 society pages and iPhone fixed five-item navigation.
- Retains 3.10 authentication flow and five verified scoring formats.
Note: membership permissions are still app-level in the current shared ggc_state architecture; server-side RLS enforcement remains a future normalized-database improvement.
