GGC Build 3.6.1 — Guest Round + Group Formats Fix

Fixes:
- Guest golfers remain in live player state during realtime cloud merges.
- Guest golfers added to a competition immediately appear in live entrant, pairing and round/group selectors.
- Manage Competition uses the current competition state rather than a stale modal snapshot.
- Saving Manage Competition no longer overwrites newly-added guest entrants with an older entrant list.
- Initial cloud/profile hydration merges with local state rather than blindly replacing newer local additions.
- Guest name placeholder changed from “e.g. Hooky” to “Name”.
- Group competition category restored in Create Competition, including Scramble/Texas Scramble, Florida Scramble, Shamble, Best 1/2/3, Cha Cha Cha, Irish Fourball, Bowmaker, Alliance and Yellow Ball.
- Existing verified-scoring safeguard retained for formats whose scoring engine is not yet enabled.
